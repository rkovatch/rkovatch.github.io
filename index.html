CRYPTOGRAPHY

Registration-Based Encryption for Internet Protocols

A new encryption scheme called registration-based encryption (RBE), developed within the last decade, allows for the binding of public keys to identities without certificates. This presents a new avenue for avoiding the traditional pitfalls of public-key infrastructure. My Master's thesis aimed to evaluate whether present RBE constructions could be applied to Internet protocols at scale. This included producing a proof-of-concept encrypted DNS protocol called ReDNS -- the first of its kind.
